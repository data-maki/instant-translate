Additional **uncommitted** fixes from this review:

- Async speaker review allocates fresh IDs, including on repeated reviews.
  Uncovered live turns cannot collide with an async provider's matching number or
  lend it a person's saved name. A regression reproduced that accidental merge.
- Native provider captions remain unknown speakers; whitespace-only IDs cannot
  group turns. Muting realtime voice stops already-buffered output.
- A late native playback completion cannot detach cancellation from the next
  audio stream. The regression failed before the one-line ownership check.
- The actual Soniox-backed realtime route passes the selected target language
  to voice output instead of defaulting to Japanese.
- Web saved-conversation duration uses explicit milliseconds. A short live test
  exposed a spurious `2h08m`; the corrected recording/reopen showed `10s`.
- Native Stop/save testing waits for the Stop frame explicitly instead of racing
  a fixed number of scheduler yields. Setup docs now separate web/backend env
  files, explain internal authless phone testing, and require a bundled release URL.

## Remaining physical-device acceptance

1. **Capture and persistence:** on the registered, unlocked iPhone, record two
   known people alternating English and the local language, including a language
   switch by the same person and one overlapping turn. Note who spoke each turn.
   Stop, confirm a topic title and complete translation, relaunch, and reopen.
   Review attribution against those notes; do not accept speaker-count agreement
   as recognition proof.
2. **Built-in speaker:** play both languages of a saved paragraph and a paragraph
   over 1,500 characters. Confirm audible original-language speech through the
   last sentence. Enable autospeak: English-to-local replies stay ordered;
   translated English stays silent. Interrupt with manual playback, disable
   autospeak, then use New chat: no stale audio/backlog returns. In realtime mode,
   Voice Off must cut buffered output. Check that TTS is not re-transcribed as
   microphone speech.
3. **Bluetooth:** repeat a short listen/speak/interrupt exchange on the intended
   headset, then disconnect/reconnect it. Confirm capture and output follow the
   intended route, recover, and remain cancellable without feedback.

These checks establish device behavior. They do not establish general diarization
accuracy; retain the attributed recording before making any stronger claim.
